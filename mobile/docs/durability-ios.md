# iOS capture durability (T11)

`CaptureCore` keeps `voice-notes/account-state.json` in the library's serial transaction. A state change is acknowledged only after the temporary file and renamed directory have been synced. A wave-1 `UserDefaults` value migrates once as `transitioning`; a cold capture remains unowned until ready reasserts `signed_in`. `start()` reads this record. `exo.debug.failAccountState` can force an account write error in Debug.

`beginRemoteOp` writes an open receipt before provider dispatch. `recordRemoteResult` updates that receipt even after the frontend account generation changes. Delete writes a tombstone, transfers receipts to deterministic `<id>:<opId>` outbox entries, then removes the sidecar last. Launch recovery repeats an interrupted transfer before removing artifacts. Outbox results are `done`, `retry`, `lookup`, `unknown`, and `authority_expired`.

A paused session has no microphone input. At launch a pause younger than 60 minutes is adopted for in-app Resume; an older pause is committed with `exitReason: pause_timeout`. The Debug override `exo.voiceNotes.pauseIdleLimitMs` may lower the limit. A recovery failure emits `recoveryFailed`. After recovery fails on three launches, the journal and segments move to `quarantine/<id>.session` rather than being retried forever. A later launch re-emits `recoveryFailed` for quarantined sessions. `retryRecovery({id})` restores one for an explicit attempt; `discardFailedRecording({id})` removes a failed session.

Verification on this branch: CaptureCore and SttCore `swift test`, ExoCapture XCTest and a supervised iPhone 16 Pro iOS 26 simulator smoke. Physical iPhone checks are deferred to TC-837: five SIGKILLs with ≤1 s loss each; five forced restarts during 10-minute recordings and one battery drain, measuring loss against spoken markers and the ≤10 s target; SIGKILL while paused then in-app Resume of the same note; pause beyond a two-minute Debug idle limit then relaunch and `pause_timeout`; sign out then immediate forced restart and an unowned cold quick-action recording; measure full-flush latency, real tap buffer sizes and audio lost at Pause.
