# iOS capture durability (T11)

`CaptureCore` keeps `voice-notes/account-state.json` in the library's serial transaction. A state change is acknowledged only after the temporary file and renamed directory have been synced. A wave-1 `UserDefaults` value migrates once as `transitioning`; a cold capture remains unowned until ready reasserts `signed_in`. `start()` reads this record. In Debug, set the string `exo.debug.failAccountState` to `1` to fail the `transitioning` write, `3` to fail the `signed_out` write, or `compensation` to fail a `signed_in` write following `transitioning`. Other writes succeed.

`beginRemoteOp` writes an open receipt before provider dispatch. `recordRemoteResult` updates that receipt even after the frontend account generation changes. Delete writes a tombstone, transfers receipts to deterministic `<id>:<opId>` outbox entries, then removes the sidecar last. Launch recovery repeats an interrupted transfer before removing artifacts. Outbox results are `done`, `retry`, `lookup`, `unknown`, and `authority_expired`.

A paused session has no microphone input. At launch a pause younger than 60 minutes is adopted for in-app Resume; an older pause is committed with `exitReason: pause_timeout`. The Debug override `exo.voiceNotes.pauseIdleLimitMs` may lower the limit. Recovery durably marks each attempt in flight before touching the session, so a process crash counts toward the three-launch limit. A new recovery failure emits `recoveryFailed`. At the limit, the journal and segments move to `quarantine/<id>.session` rather than being retried forever. Later scans leave quarantined audio untouched and do not repeat the event. `retryRecovery({id})` restores one for an explicit attempt; `discardFailedRecording({id})` requires a failed session, tombstones it and removes its audio. A healthy session rejects discard with `not_failed_recording`.

The first delivered PCM tap buffer journals `first_audio`, and an engine/input stop journals `capture_stopped` after tap callbacks drain. Their wall times become nullable `firstAudioAt` and `captureStoppedAt` in the sidecar, including recovered notes. A discharging battery at or below 5 percent journals `low_battery.level` as an integer percent.

Verification on this branch: CaptureCore and SttCore `swift test`, ExoCapture XCTest and a supervised iPhone 16 Pro iOS 26 simulator smoke. Physical iPhone checks are deferred to TC-837: five SIGKILLs with ≤1 s loss each; five forced restarts during 10-minute recordings and one battery drain, measuring loss against spoken markers and the ≤10 s target; SIGKILL while paused then in-app Resume of the same note; pause beyond a two-minute Debug idle limit then relaunch and `pause_timeout`; sign out then immediate forced restart and an unowned cold quick-action recording; measure full-flush latency, real tap buffer sizes and audio lost at Pause.
