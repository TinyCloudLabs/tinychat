Keep on-device recordings whose transcript was never saved, whether Exo quit or crashed or the Local recording view closed mid-recording. Local recording offers the recording again with Transcribe or Discard, and Transcribe saves it as a normal Exo Local meeting. A kept recording belongs to the account that recorded it, and one whose audio file is gone can only be discarded (TC-770).
