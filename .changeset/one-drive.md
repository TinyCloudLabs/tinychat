One Apps lists Drive: your TinyCloud as drives, folders and files (TinyDrive), framed in One and read only. One hands Drive one drive at a time, when you open it: the data your apps keep, the files in your own space, or what your agents keep. Drive gets no write, no delete, no SQL, no secrets and not the list of your grants; its "Who has access" opens Drive in its own tab. The phone app does not frame Drive yet. Explorer stays in the list for now.
