TrustBot is always offered its meeting tools and its feed and chat tools, also while the owner has that access off. When a question needs meetings or the feed and chats and that access is off, TrustBot tries the read, the read is refused as before, and TrustBot tells the owner in one short sentence that it needs that access. The attempt lets the app offer the owner that access at that moment. A refused read reads nothing, does not count against the day's reads and does not pause web access. The off messages now say only that the owner can turn the access on in this chat. Telegram tools and Courier turns are unchanged.
