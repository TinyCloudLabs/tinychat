A Feed card has an address: `tinycloud.one/chat/feed/<card id>`, and the shorter `tinycloud.one/feed/<card id>`. Opening it (signed out, you sign in first and land on the same card) shows the Feed with that card open and outlined for a moment, under History or Snoozed if that is where it lives. If the card is not in your feed (removed, expired, or in someone else's feed) the Feed opens as usual with a short note you can dismiss. Details: TC-3279.
