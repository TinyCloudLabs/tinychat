Word fixes: a list in Settings of words to replace in transcripts, for example "tiny cloud" becomes "TinyCloud". Add, edit or delete a pair; matching ignores case, matches whole words and phrases, and the replacement is inserted as typed. The list is saved in your space so it follows you across devices, and it applies to every transcript you read, copy or export, including notes saved earlier. The stored transcript is not changed.
