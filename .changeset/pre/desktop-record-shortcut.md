TinyCloud Home for Mac starts a recording from anywhere: press ⌘⇧⌥R (Command-Shift-Option-R), or choose Start recording from its menu-bar icon. The app comes to the front with the recorder open and recording; if a recording is already going, it opens that recorder instead. The menu-bar icon also has Open TinyCloud Home.
