New setting on the Mac: Read names from the call window, in Capture settings under Meetings. It is off until you turn it on. While a recording of both sides of a call runs, TinyCloud One reads the names of the people in the call from the Google Meet or Zoom window and saves them with the note: they are offered when you rename a speaker, and with one other person in the call that name is suggested for Them. It needs Also record this Mac's audio, so it sits under that switch: turning it on turns that on too, and turning that off (or choosing Mic only for your first recording) turns it off. macOS asks for Accessibility permission; until it is allowed the setting shows Open System Settings. Saving a note never waits for the names. Nothing changes on the web or on phones.
