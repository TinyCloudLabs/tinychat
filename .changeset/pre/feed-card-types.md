Feed: cards can show what kind of task they are, and messages from another team over Courier are marked. An agent can label a card Spend, Publish, Send, Access, Rule, Ship, Do, Review, Direction, Answer, News or Alert. The label shows at the top right of the card in the list and Cards views, and beside the title of an open card. Spend, Publish, Send and Access, where your tap sends something out, are outlined with an arrow; Alert uses the warning colour; the rest are grey. A message relayed over Courier starts with the Courier mark and its sender, and the open card adds "via" the agent that passed it on. The mark appears only on cards that come from your Courier connection. Filter offers "Task" when any card has a label. A label the app doesn't recognise doesn't change the card and is listed in Details as not shown.
