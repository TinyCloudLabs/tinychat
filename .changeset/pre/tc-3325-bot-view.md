The Chat tab can show a bot's own chat in place of the classic chat, behind a "Chat view" choice kept per account on each device. Classic stays the default, so nothing changes unless an account picks Bots and is let into bots. The bot view has the same composer as Main's chat, keeps a reply going when you switch bots, saves history into your space the same way, and offers Try again when a reply fails.
