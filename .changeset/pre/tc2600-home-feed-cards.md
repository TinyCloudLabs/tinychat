Home's feed is now a list of cards. Every card shows at once, grouped under "Needs you" and "Earlier", with Needs you, All and Done above them and a filter for agent, project and type. A question answers with one tap on an option, or in your own words. The answer waits 5 seconds with an Undo button, then it's sent. Close the page during those 5 seconds and the answer is still sent, at once or the next time you open Home, unless the request changed in between. Comments sit under each card, one reply deep, as on the sitrep board, and an agent's replies show there too. The private note is gone from the card. Cards use plain words throughout. Empty and loading states are cards, and the signed-out demo uses the same card.
