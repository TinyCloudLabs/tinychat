Rename the speakers on any transcript. Tap a speaker label in a note's transcript to give that speaker a name, for example "Me" becomes your name and "Them" becomes "Alice, Bob". The name shows on every line of that speaker, is saved with the note so it follows it across devices, and can be undone. Copy, copy with timestamps and Markdown export use the names. The stored transcript keeps its original labels. Details: TC-2189
