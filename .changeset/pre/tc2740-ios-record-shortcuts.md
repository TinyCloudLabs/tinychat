iOS: TinyCloud Home ships recording shortcuts that work right after install (TC-2740). Siri, Spotlight, the Shortcuts app and the Action button get **Record** ("Start recording in TinyCloud Home", "Record a note in TinyCloud Home") and **Stop Recording**; Control Center and the Lock Screen get a **Record a Voice Note** control that records without unlocking; the home-screen quick action uses the same start. Every recording shows a Live Activity with a timer and a Stop button. The test-only "TinyCloud Home probe" control and shortcut are gone.
