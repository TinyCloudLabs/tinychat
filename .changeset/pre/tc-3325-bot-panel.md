In the bot view, a small line between the messages says what the bot did on its computer, such as "Ran 3 commands" or "Changed 1 file", and it is still there after a reload. The bot's name at the top opens a panel with the bot's avatar, its activity with times (newest first), and where it runs, such as "Private · asleep". The panel is a column beside the chat on wide screens and a sheet on smaller ones. From the panel you can change a bot's name, shape and colour, or delete the bot after a confirmation (Main can't be deleted).
