A Mac voice note says what it is doing from Stop to its transcript. While its audio is finished on the Mac it shows "Finishing the recording…", never "Waiting to upload" or a transcript that waits for your space. Capture shows "Saving to your TinyCloud space…" while the note saves. A note with no Whisper model shows that it waits for a model and offers Get model, never "Couldn't transcribe". A reload shows the same state again, and the note's transcription starts only once.
