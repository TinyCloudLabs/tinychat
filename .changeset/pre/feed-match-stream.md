The Feed is one list: Needs you, Updates, Pending and History, one under the other, each with a heading that stays in view while you scroll its section. The island at the top shows Needs you, Updates and History with their counts. Tap one to jump to it, and the highlight follows as you scroll. An empty section is dimmed on the island and can't be tapped. When nothing needs you, Needs you still comes first and says so. The old Needs you, All and History views are gone.
