An on-device transcript from a single-channel recording saves its speaker as "Speaker 1", an anonymous name for that note only, instead of "You" or no name, and lists it as the note's participant. Each engine segment stays its own timed record. Two-channel desktop labels and private-cloud speaker labels are unchanged. Notes transcribed by AssemblyAI say that its speaker labels apply within that note only, and the retrieval pack (`0.1.1-onboarding.23`) documents the same for agents.
