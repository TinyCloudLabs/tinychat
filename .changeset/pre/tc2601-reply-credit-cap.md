Stop a chat reply at the credit limit and never charge above it (TC-2601). Backend-orchestrated replies cap every provider call's `max_tokens` from the allowance the account had when the reply started and end with `budget_exhausted` instead of overshooting; all chat paths (agent chat, Eliza tasks, `/api/chat`) now settle through one clamp: a reply is charged at most its starting allowance and at most what is left of the window it started in, a failed reply is charged only when the model's own output broke it (a tool it was not offered, tool calls that never finished or did not parse, output over its cap) and is otherwise free even after content was delivered, and a timed-out reply is charged at most its starting allowance. The ledger record id is now the reply's execution id. Eliza `/tasks` monitor modes are unchanged. Customer-visible effect: replies near the limit can end early with the existing "reached the limit" notice, overshoot is platform cost, not the user's, and provider or server failures cost nothing. See `docs/credits.md`.
