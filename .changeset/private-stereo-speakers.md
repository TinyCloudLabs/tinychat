Private transcripts of Mac recordings tell apart the people on the other side of a call, where the Private service offers it. A call with several remote speakers shows them as Them 1, Them 2 and so on in place of one "Them"; your own side stays "Me", and a call with one other person still reads "Me" and "Them". Where the service does not offer it, the notes read "Me" and "Them" as before, and Identify speakers shows as not supported for a Mac recording that includes the Mac's audio.
