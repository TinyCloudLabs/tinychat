TinyCloud Home on iPhone and Android now opens with Face ID, Touch ID or the phone's screen lock instead of signing in again. A signed-in app asks when it opens, and when you come back after 5 minutes away (Settings › App lock: 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or an hour). Your feed and recordings load behind the lock screen and appear the moment you unlock; the app switcher shows a blank screen while the lock is on. Cancelling keeps the lock screen up with a button to try again, and never signs you out. The lock is on by default where Face ID, Touch ID or a fingerprint is set up, and can be turned off in Settings. Recording from a shortcut starts without waiting for the unlock. Details: TC-2738
