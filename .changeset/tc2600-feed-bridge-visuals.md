Home's feed brings in parts of Hunter's bridge-feed design (TC-2600). Each agent has its own coloured letter mark. A platform name shows beside it when the card reports one. Tapping the agent's name shows what it is, and "Open conversation" opens its page: its cards, your answers and the comments, in time order. From there you can reach the list of your agents. An answer now shows its steps under it: Sent, the agent received it, then Done or Failed. Four switches let us compare the rest of the design. Each is off by default, so the feed stays as approved. They are one open card with the others as rows, a view menu with Agents and Filter, a 4-second undo, and no caption under the comment control. A build sets them with VITE_FEED_LAYOUT, VITE_FEED_NAV, VITE_FEED_UNDO and VITE_FEED_CAPTIONS. On a preview deployment the address can set them too, for example ?feed.layout=open-card, or ?feed=default to reset. The feed marks any switch that is not at its default.
