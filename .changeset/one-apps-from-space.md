One Apps: Drive runs from the owner's own space through the app runner, with no site of its own. One reads the app's files from the Apps space, checks them against the app's release record and hands them to the runner at the app's own sub-address.

Drive's tile shows only for an owner who installed it in their Apps space, and Apps checks again on each visit, so an app installed while One is open shows with no new sign-in. While Drive runs inside One, its bar offers no new tab (it has no page outside One); where One can't frame it, its screen offers One on the web.
