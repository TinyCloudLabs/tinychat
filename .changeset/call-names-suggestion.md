In a Mac recording of a call with one other person, Them shows that person's name, marked Suggested, when the desktop app read the names from the call window. Save the name unchanged to keep it, rename it, or choose Go back to Them. A name you already gave is never replaced, and a call with more people suggests nothing. This also works while your TinyCloud space cannot be reached: a note that is so far only on this Mac shows the suggestion and offers the names, and what you choose is saved with the note when it reaches your space.
