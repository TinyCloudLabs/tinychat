Feed: the Feed is one list. Needs you, Updates, Pending and History sit one under the other, each under a heading that stays at the top while its section scrolls. The island at the top is a map of the list: Needs you, Updates and History, each with its count. A tap scrolls to that section, and scrolling moves the highlight. A section with nothing in it is off on the island. When nothing needs you, Needs you still comes first and says so. There is room under the last section, so it can come up to the top. The old Needs you, All and History views are gone; History is the end of the list.
