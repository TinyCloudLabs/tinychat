A card in the Home feed has one place to write, labelled with who gets it: "Reply to Studio agent". A reply goes to the card's owner when you approved a link for it, otherwise to the agent that posted the card; only those receive it, and nothing is saved "unrouted". Sending counts down with Undo like an answer ("Sending to Studio agent in 4s"), then the message shows in the card's conversation with its steps. A card with no agent that can take a reply shows no box and one line that says so. A note to yourself is offered only inside Ask me later. Ids, routing and the card reference move behind Details, each card shows one status word (Needs you, Waiting on <agent>, Done, or Snoozed, Expired, Closed), and a card from a crew member names it, "via" the agent that posted it. The folded row after an answer shows the conversation's latest line and a reply. Tapping an agent's name, or choosing it from the new Agents list, shows everything between you and that agent in time order, with a reply box. The agent, type and project filters and History sit behind one Filter control. The feed lab adds a card whose owner has an approved link, and its simulated agent answers a reply after about two seconds.
