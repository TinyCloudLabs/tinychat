Deleting an account stops Calendar autojoin and removes its Google sign-in from our server, removes the Fireflies import grant, the connector grant and the exo sync grant that our server holds, and stops meeting notifications. Open tabs in this browser that hold the same sign-in stop writing the transcript grant again, and this device signs out as OpenKey opens. A step that cannot be confirmed is shown with Try again and Continue, and the text says what may remain. The record of deleted meetings and the owner's space are not erased.
