On a phone, an app TinyCloud One opens inside One (Explorer, Account, Courier, Ledger) fills the whole screen: no app bar and no navigation pill. A small round frosted-glass Back button floats in the top-left corner, below the status bar, and returns to where you were in One. The browser's and the system's Back do the same. The Apps grid keeps its scroll position when you come back. Tablets and desktops keep the app bar and navigation as before.
