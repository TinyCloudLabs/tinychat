Exo mobile voice notes can be transcribed with private cloud transcription (TinyCloud Private Transcription → Tinfoil), the same path as the desktop's Exo Local engine. It is offered only when the build has a PTX upload origin and the backend admits the account, after a one-time "Use private cloud" confirmation. New notes are transcribed after they are saved, and older ones have a Transcribe action. The card shows progress, failures with Retry, and the transcript. Transcripts appear in Library, and voice notes are now part of the meeting chat corpus.
