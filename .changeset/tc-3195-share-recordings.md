You can share a meeting or voice note recording with a link. People who open it sign in, find it under Shared with me in their Library, and can read the transcript and play the audio until 30 days after you create the link, or until you stop sharing. You choose how long the link can be opened: 1, 7 or 30 days. On iOS and Android, tinycloud.one/s/ links open in the app.
