Feed on a phone: one row is open at a time. Opening a row folds the one that was open, the other rows dim while it is open, and the open row comes to just under the section heading, growing open with its parts rising in one after the other. A "Show less" at the foot of an open card folds it from where you stopped reading and brings its row back into view.
