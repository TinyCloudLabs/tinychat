Stopping a recording automatically is now a setting, "Stop recordings automatically" (per device). It is off by default at the phone layout, where a recording runs until you stop it (with a silent eight-hour safety cap) and shows no countdown or warning, and on by default on desktop, where Exo asks "Keep recording?" five minutes before the limit and "Keep going" adds an hour. Recordings too long for private transcription are saved with their audio kept, and the note now says so.
