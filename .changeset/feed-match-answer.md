Feed: answering a card takes it out of the list at once, with one pill at the top of the list that names what you answered, with Undo and a five-second countdown. A second answer sends the first at once, and the pill then says where it went ("Sent to" and the agent's name). Acknowledge, Drop and Later use the same pill and never say "Sent to". Counts drop when you answer and come back if you Undo. An answered card waits in Pending at the end of the list ("You chose …", with the post and "Waiting on" the agent's name); Pending is always open and a tap on a row opens the card again, where Change answer lives. A card that asks now has "See N options" under its row, which opens the same card as tapping the post and turns into "Hide options" while it is open.
