The Mac's Record this call tile now stays while the recording it started runs, as a small bar: a red dot that pulses gently (still and dimmed while paused), the elapsed time, and Stop, which stops and saves the note as Stop in the app does. A newly shown tile ignores clicks for half a second, so a click meant for the call does not start a recording. The app answers the tile's Stop only while a recording is live or paused, through the recorder's own Stop.
