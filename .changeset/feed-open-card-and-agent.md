The open card in the Home feed is one raised surface: the agent line, the question, its options, the reply box and the small actions (Ask me later or Snooze, Next card, Details) sit inside it, with a clear gap before the rest. Options read as choices: filled rows with a round marker, the label, its detail line and "Suggested" on the one the agent recommends; the one-tap countdown, Undo and fold are unchanged. The other cards in the view form a list under plain headings with a count, "Also needs you · 3" and "Updates · 1": one row each with the agent's mark, the title on one line, and the agent's name and platform. Each agent has its own mark, its first letter in its own colour, the same on its cards, in the list, on its page and in the Agents list. A card's first line names its agent and, when the card's payload carries an optional `agent` object (`{ kind, model, runsOn }`), a chip with the platform's name and, for Claude, Gemini, Cursor, Perplexity and OpenCode, its mark (files from Simple Icons in `public/platforms`); without that object there is no chip. Tapping the agent's name shows what it is, its model and where it runs as it reports them, and what it can do, with a way to its conversation; the same facts head the agent's page. Times are short ("9:02", or the day and time on another day). "Mark private priority" moves under Details, and a note to yourself saved with Ask me later is shown on the card when it comes back. The feed lab's agents report Codex, Grok, Claude and Muse, and one reports nothing.
