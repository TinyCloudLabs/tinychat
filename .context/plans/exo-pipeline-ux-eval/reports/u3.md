# U3: phone Stop and transcription responsiveness

## Changes

- iOS hands Stop to a worker before stopping input, draining AAC, syncing its checkpoint, or deactivating the audio session. The existing commit and Stop promise still complete after mux. A guarded background task ends both on normal completion and on expiry. Local STT resumes after mux publishes the audio.
- Android's Stop already uses a worker thread. Its STT loop now emits a changed whole percent at most every 250 ms, with a terminal 100% update. `windowsDone` and `segmentsDone` share a checkpoint at most every five seconds during decoding, plus one final checkpoint.
