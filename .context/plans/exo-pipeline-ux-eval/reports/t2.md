# T2: fine transcript segments

## Changes

- Mac Whisper persists final streamed VAD ranges for mono and stereo notes. When those events are unavailable, word-derived bounds are marked coarse; stereo channel identity is retained.
- Apple Speech retains final phrase ranges and marks proportional splits of ranges over 30 seconds coarse. Android retains its original VAD offsets and marks them engine timed.
- PTX persists its own regions rather than merging them into turns. AssemblyAI uses its sentence endpoint and word times for long sentences, retaining numeric channels and speaker IDs. Long ranges without usable word timing are split with coarse provenance.
- New local transcripts use version 2. Version 1 transcripts remain readable and retain their legacy speaker label on a later sync.
